## Everything you need to keep progressing.

Gravl tracks your recovery, plans your week, and adapts every workout to the gym you are in. It takes care of the details that stall progress, so you can just enjoy lifting.

### Gravl already planned your whole week.

While your coach is still typing, Gravl has your week built: every set, every rep, every kilo, progressive overload included, recalculated after every workout you log. No guessing, no walls of text, no questions left on read.

**Import workouts from anywhere.**Got a routine on Instagram or TikTok, a photo of a whiteboard, or a PDF from your coach? Drop it into Gravl and AI turns it into a real workout: every set, rep and weight ready to track in your next session.
