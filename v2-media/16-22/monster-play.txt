## About this app

_arrow\_forward_

Muscle Monster: Your AI Workout Planner for Home & Gym  
  
Build muscle, lose weight, and get stronger with Muscle Monster, your smart fitness coach and personalized workout planner. Whether you're starting your fitness journey or building on your progress, discover home and gym workout routines designed for your goals, fitness level, and available equipment.  
  
PERSONALIZED WORKOUT PLANS  
No more guessing what to train next. Choose your fitness goal and preferences to get a structured workout plan that fits your lifestyle.  
  
• Muscle-building workouts for strength and progress  
• Weight loss workout plans to support your fitness goals  
• Full-body workout routines for balanced training  
• Beginner-friendly exercises and advanced training options  
• Personalized plans for home or gym workouts  
  
HOME & GYM WORKOUTS  
Train at home, in the gym, or wherever your day takes you. Muscle Monster helps you find workouts that fit your schedule and equipment.  
  
• Home workouts with little or no equipment  
• Gym workout routines for muscle building and strength  
• Bodyweight and calisthenics exercises  
• Dumbbell workouts for home and gym training  
• Upper-body, lower-body, abs, and full-body workouts  
  
Whether you prefer short home sessions or a structured gym workout routine, you can train your way.  
  
DUMBBELL & STRENGTH TRAINING  
Build strength with guided dumbbell workouts and personalized training routines. Explore exercises for your chest, back, shoulders, arms, legs, and core. Follow clear exercise demonstrations and work toward better consistency, whether you're using dumbbells at home or equipment at the gym.  
  
WEIGHT LOSS, ABS & FULL-BODY FITNESS  
Looking for effective weight loss workout plans or ways to strengthen your core? Explore full-body training, abs exercises, and fitness routines that support an active lifestyle.  
  
Combine strength workouts with regular movement to build healthier habits. Choose exercises based on your goals rather than following a one-size-fits-all program.  
  
YOUR SMART AI FITNESS COACH  
Make workout planning simpler with AI-powered training recommendations. Share your goals, fitness level, and preferences to get a personalized plan suited to your needs.  
  
From choosing your next workout to following a daily training routine, Muscle Monster helps you stay focused on what matters: showing up and making progress.  
  
300+ EXERCISES & 200+ WORKOUTS  
Discover an extensive exercise library for different muscle groups, training environments, and fitness goals.  
  
• Guided exercise demonstrations and instructions  
• Bodyweight, dumbbell, and gym exercises  
• Strength, core, and full-body workout options  
• Routines for beginners and experienced users  
  
Explore new movements, find workouts that suit your equipment, and build confidence with every session.  
  
21-DAY WORKOUT CHALLENGE  
Build a more consistent routine with a structured 21-day workout challenge. Follow your plan, complete training sessions, and develop habits you can continue beyond the challenge.  
  
TRACK YOUR WORKOUT PROGRESS  
Keep your fitness journey organized. Record completed workouts, review your training history, and follow your progress over time. Small steps add up when you stick with a routine.  
  
GET STARTED WITH MUSCLE MONSTER  
1. Choose your fitness goal: build muscle, lose weight, or improve strength.  
2. Share your fitness level and personal preferences.  
3. Select home or gym training and your available equipment.  
4. Follow your personalized workout plan.  
5. Track your workouts and keep moving toward your goals.  
  
Muscle Monster brings home workouts, gym training, dumbbell routines, and smart fitness planning together in one app.  
  
Train at home. Get stronger at the gym. Find a workout routine that works for you.

Home & gym workouts, weight loss plans, dumbbell training & an AI coach.
