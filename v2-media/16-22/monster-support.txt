How can I check the status of my premium/subscription?

Currently, the App does not support viewing subscription records or membership expiration dates. If needed, please log in to your account via the website at https://webapp.musclemonster.fit. Once logged in, go to the "Settings" option, then select "Subscription" to view and manage your subscription.

Can I use my subscription on multiple devices?

Yes. If you want to use the premium on a different device, please use the same login way to log in. First, please go to the "Me" page of the device that has the premium account, and click the setting icon. Second, click the option for Log Out. If you don't set the email address and password, the system will remind you to set one. And then you can log in to the premium account with the same email address and password.

Where can I find courses for women?

We have recently launched several women’s training programs in the app. You can view them after updating your gender in your "Me" page, or find them in the plan section under the Home and Gym tabs on the Library page.And We will continue to launch more women-focused programs to better meet the needs of our users. We would also like to share that the core training principles and muscle structure are not fundamentally different between men and women. Although most of our earlier programs were designed with male users in mind, women can achieve equally effective results—such as improving strength, enhancing posture, and boosting overall fitness—by following the same training routines. If you have any suggestions for women’s programs, we’d love to hear from you. We’re committed to continually improving and expanding our content!

How do I set up the plan I want?

We suggest that you use the Custom tab for your workout. In the AI Coach section, you can select the time, training location, target body parts, goals, and the equipment you have. Account Settings

![Image 39](https://dailyyogaappimage.dailyyoga.com/99/28/99283c6c7a41bf44047a7b37fdf000cd.webp)

My Premium is lost/I purchased Premium, but the app doesn't recognise it.

For iOS users, please go to Me>Settings>Restore Purchase to locate your Premium account. After the Premium account is being located, please log out of the current account and make sure to log in with the correct account to access the Premium. For both iOS and Android users, you can try to log in a different way, such as FB, Apple/Google ID and email address. The Premium may be associated with another account other than the account you have logged into already.

![Image 40](https://dailyyogaappimage.dailyyoga.com/99/28/99283c6c7a41bf44047a7b37fdf000cd.webp)

What should I do if I forget my password when logging into my account?

If you forget your password, please tap "Forget Password" or "Passwordless Login" on the Login screen to reset password for your Muscle Monster account. If you choose the option with "Forget Password", you will receive a password reset email and you can change the password through it directly. If you choose the option with "Passwordless Login", you will receive a new email with the Verification code. Then the account can be log in with the code. The verification code will expire on the second login and you will need to obtain it again.
