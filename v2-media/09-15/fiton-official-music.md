Title: PRO: How can I adjust music during a workout?

Markdown Content:
All workouts on FitOn include an "Original" royalty-free music station. The selected tracks match the energy and tempo of the workout.

If you want to hear the latest chart-toppers and hits from top artists, you can upgrade to **[FitOn PRO](https://fitonapp.com/pro/)**. Users who upgrade will have access to 8 Premium Music stations, giving them complete control over their workout music experience.

**3. Why can’t I play Premium Music through Chromecast with my iPhone?**

Premium Music works with Apple TV via Airplay but not with Chromecast due to limitations on that platform. When casting to Chromecast the workout defaults back to playing our original music station. As soon as the platform is updated to allow us to stream both the workout video & premium music, we will update our apps to add support.
