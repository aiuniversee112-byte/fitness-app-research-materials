# FitOn

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

Registration asks for name and email; no password field is shown in the recorded form. Body measurements belong to one About You form.

- Registration — What is your name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Basic profile — What is your gender?
- Basic profile — What is your date of birth?
- Body data — What is your height?
- Body data — What is your weight?
- Fitness goal — What's your goal?
- Fitness level — Choose your fitness level.
- Training frequency — How many times per week do you plan to work out?
- Workout duration — How much time do you have to work out?
- Class preferences — Select all your favorite types of classes.
- Community — Which community groups would you like to join?
- Devices — Select all the devices you own.
- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
