# Gymdone

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — initial onboarding

English translations of Russian prompts. The recorded branch has no painful joints. Account connection appears after the trial purchase; no separate email-registration form is shown.

- Basic profile — What is your gender?
- Fitness goal — What is your goal?
- Fitness level — What is your fitness level?
- Basic profile — What is your age range?
- Body data — What is your height?
- Body data — How much do you weigh now?
- Weight goal — What is your target weight?
- Training frequency — How many days per week would you like to train?
- Workout duration — What is your preferred workout duration?
- Training location — Where will you train?
- Equipment — What equipment do you have?
- Joint pain — Do you have any painful joints?
- Commitment — Confirm your promise to train at the selected weekly frequency.
- Apple Health — Would you like to sync your workouts and active calories with Apple Health?
- Registration — Continue with Apple, Google or email to save your plan.

### User recording — first-use setup

Additional requests shown during initial rest-timer use and creation of a custom workout.

- Rest reminders — Enable notifications when your rest period ends?
- Custom workout — Would you like to create a workout plan or an individual workout?
- Exercise selection — Which exercises would you like to add?
