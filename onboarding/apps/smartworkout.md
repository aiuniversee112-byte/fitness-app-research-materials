# SmartWorkout

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — Quick start and registration

English translations of Russian prompts. The recorded route is the 10-question Quick start; the longer coach-style interview is offered but not entered.

- Onboarding route — Choose Quick start, Coach-style interview, or Skip plan.
- Basic profile — What is your gender?
- Fitness goal — What is your main goal?
- Training experience — How long have you been training?
- Training frequency — How many days per week can you train?
- Workout duration — How long does one workout last?
- Training location — Where do you train?
- Training split — How would you like to split your workouts?
- Muscle priorities — Which muscle groups do you most want to develop? Choose up to two, or skip.
- Exercise exclusions — Which exercises would you prefer to avoid?
- Recovery — How is your recovery, considering sleep, stress and daily fatigue?
- Workout import — Have you trained in another app? Paste 1–3 recent workouts, or start from scratch.
- Plan generation — Choose the AI model for your plan.
- Registration — Continue with Apple, Google, or without an account.
- Apple sign-in — Confirm the name to share with SmartWorkout.
- Apple sign-in — Would you like to share or hide your email address?
- Public profile — What should people call you? Choose a nickname.

### User recording — first-workout setup invitation

Shown after subscription activation. The additional workout-log questionnaire is not opened in the recording.

- Workout log — Set up your workout log before your first workout, or proceed to start training.
