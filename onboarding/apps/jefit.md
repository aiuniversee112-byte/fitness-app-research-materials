# JEFIT

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The personal-plan offer precedes account creation. Questionnaire prompts are followed by the email registration form.

- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
- Basic profile — Select your gender.
- Fitness goal — What's your main goal for training?
- Current physique — Which describes your current build best?
- Target physique — What is your goal body type?
- Target areas — What are your target zones?
- Fitness level — What is your fitness level?
- Body data — What is your height?
- Body data — What is your current weight?
- Weight goal — What is your goal weight?
- Basic profile — What is your age?
- Training location — What's your preferred place to work out?
- Equipment — Select your equipment list.
- Training frequency — How many times per week would you like to train?
- Workout duration — What is a perfect length of a workout for you?
- Injuries — Do you have any injuries? Select the affected muscles or joints.
- Training confidence — How often do you guess your weight or reps?
- Notifications — Allow JEFIT to send notifications?
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google, Facebook or email to create your account.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Choose a username.
- Registration — Create a password that meets the displayed requirements.
- Password storage — Would you like to save your password for autofill?

### Appllama recording — first-workout setup

Shown after account creation, during the first workout-building and logging sequence.

- Workout setup — Create a workout from scratch or find a plan.
- Workout setup — Add a workout day.
- Exercise selection — Which exercises would you like to add to your workout?
- Workout logging — Enter the number of repetitions for each set, then log the set.
