# TRX

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — registration, trial and personalization

The recorded route creates an account and activates a trial before the fitness questionnaire. Birthday and gender are optional in the registration form.

- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password with at least 6 characters.
- Registration — Confirm your password.
- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your date of birth? (Optional)
- Registration — What is your gender? (Optional)
- Trial activation — Activate the free trial?
- Notifications — Would you like notifications, class reminders and important updates?
- Personalization — Would you like to customize your TRX Training experience?
- Fitness level — What best describes your fitness level?
- Training frequency — How often do you work out?
- Workout duration — What length of workouts do you prefer?
- Workout intensity — How intense do you prefer your workouts to be?
- Training goals — What are your training goals?
- Suspension setup — How do you usually anchor your Suspension Trainer?
- Special interests — Is there a sport, activity or specialty you would like to target with your training?
- Equipment — What fitness equipment do you usually have available?
- Tracking permission — Allow TRX to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?
- Local network — Allow TRX to find Cast-enabled devices on your local network?
