# Gymverse

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The app counter includes informational slides. Repeated age, height and weight edits are listed only once.

- Basic profile — What's your gender?
- Fitness goal — What's your goal?
- Training challenges — What is your major challenge at the gym?
- Plan focus — Choose a balanced or focused plan: full body, upper body, lower body or abs.
- Training experience — How long have you been weight training?
- Training routine — Do you weight train regularly?
- Training location — Where do you work out?
- Equipment — Which equipment do you have available?
- Training frequency — How often would you like to work out?
- Schedule — Would you prefer scheduled workouts or a flexible schedule?
- Workout duration — How long do you want your workouts to be?
- Apple Health — Allow Gymverse to access and update your Health data?
- Basic profile — What's your age?
- Body data — What's your height?
- Body data — How much do you weigh?
- First workout — When would you like to schedule your first workout?
- Notifications — Allow workout reminders and training notifications?
- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
- Referral code — Do you have a referral code to enter?

### Appllama recording — first-plan feedback

Shown on the first workout screen after the free-pass offer.

- Plan feedback — Do you like your plan?
