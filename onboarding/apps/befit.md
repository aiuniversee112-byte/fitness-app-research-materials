# BeFit

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — initial onboarding

The 16-step counter includes a promotional slide. The route selects no active injuries; a follow-up injury-area selector is therefore not shown.

- Gender identity — Which gender do you identify with?
- Fitness goal — What's your current fitness goal?
- Training experience — What's your strength-training experience?
- Training routine — How often do you work out?
- Training location — Where do you work out?
- Injuries — Do you have any active injuries?
- Muscle focus — Which areas would you like to focus on?
- Apple Health — Would you like to connect to Apple Health?
- Basic profile — What's your age?
- Body data — What's your height?
- Body data — What's your weight?
- Weight goal — What's your goal weight?
- Plan setup — How would you like to set up your workouts: Smart Plan or a plan created by you?
- Workout duration — What's your ideal workout duration?
- Plan renewal — How often should your plan be renewed?
- Training frequency — How often would you like to work out?
- Notifications — Allow workout reminders, progress updates and personalized notifications?
- Registration — Sign up with Apple or continue with email to save your progress.
- Apple sign-in — Confirm the name to share with BeFit.
- Apple sign-in — Would you like to share or hide your email address?

### User recording — first-use tour

Shown after the initial onboarding and account connection.

- Product tour — Start the product tour or skip the tutorial.
- Workout mode — Choose a personalized plan, your own plan or a quick workout.
