# SmartGym

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The recorded branch uses Smart Trainer. Permissions and consent appear after the subscription offers; no registration form is shown.

- Setup mode — How do you want to use SmartGym: have workouts built for you, or create your own?
- Training experience — What is your training experience level?
- Equipment — Which equipment do you have available?
- Fitness goal — Choose your training goal: strength, muscle size, muscle endurance or HIIT.
- Schedule — Which days of the week would you like to work out?
- Workout duration — How long would you like your workouts to be?
- Consent — Do you accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy?
- Apple Health — Allow SmartGym to access and update your Health data?
- Notifications — Allow notifications for important information and rest-timer completion?

### Appllama recording — first-use feedback

Shown after reaching the Routines screen.

- Product tour — Would you like to take the app guided tour?
- App feedback — Are you liking SmartGym?
