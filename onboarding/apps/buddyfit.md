# Buddyfit

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — account setup and seven-question quiz

The fitness quiz appears after trial activation. An Apple sign-up attempt fails and the recording proceeds through Google; the visible email form is retained as an available registration route.

- Notifications — Allow Buddyfit to send notifications?
- Cookie preferences — Choose which cookie categories to accept: essential, preferences, analytics and marketing.
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google or email to create your account.
- Tracking permission — Allow Buddyfit to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?
- Email registration — What is your email address?
- Email registration — Create a password with at least 8 characters, one letter and one number.
- Google sign-in — Allow Buddyfit to use accounts.google.com to sign in?
- Gender identity — How do you identify?
- Basic profile — When's your birthday?
- Body data — What's your height?
- Body data — What's your current weight?
- Motivation — What motivates you?
- Current activity — How active are you right now?
- Weekly goal — What's your weekly workout goal?
- Recommended program — Choose a recommended training program.
- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
