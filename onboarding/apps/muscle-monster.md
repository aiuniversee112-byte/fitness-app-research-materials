# Muscle Monster

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

Muscle-building branch. The email form appears after the plan preview and the lucky-draw offer.

- Basic profile — What is your gender?
- Fitness goal — What's your fitness goal?
- Muscle-building motivation — What is your main muscle-building goal?
- Target areas — Which areas do you want to focus on?
- Body data — How old are you?
- Body data — What is your height?
- Body data — What is your current weight?
- Weight goal — What is your target weight?
- Goal pace — At what pace would you like to achieve your goal?
- Body shape — Choose your current body shape.
- Body shape — Choose your desired body shape.
- Lifestyle — Do you experience any of these issues: prolonged sitting, poor sleep quality or dietary issues?
- Injuries — Have you suffered any injuries recently?
- Fitness level — How would you rate your fitness level?
- Equipment — Choose your preferred training equipment.
- Schedule — Which days of the week would you like to train?
- Reminders — Would you like training reminders?
- Training concerns — Are you unsure how to choose an exercise method that suits you?
- Training concerns — Are you unsure about the right exercise frequency and intensity for your current physical condition?
- Motivation — Do you want a healthier body?
- Motivation — Do you want to be more confident?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Confirm your email address or change it.
