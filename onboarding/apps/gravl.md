# Gravl

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

Apple Health is skipped, so the manual body-statistics branch is shown. The subscription offer interrupts the sign-up screen before email registration resumes.

- Name — What would you like us to call you?
- Motivation — Why do you want to use a fitness app? Choose up to three reasons.
- Fitness goals — What are your top fitness goals?
- Training experience — How experienced are you with lifting weights?
- Apple Health — Allow Gravl to access Apple Health, or skip?
- Basic profile — What's your gender?
- Basic profile — How old are you? Select your date of birth.
- Body data — What's your height?
- Body data — What's your weight?
- Training frequency — How often do you want to train?
- Training split — What's your preferred workout split? Let Gravl choose, use a recovery split, or pick from the library.
- Training location — Where do you exercise?
- Equipment — Select the equipment you have access to.
- Notifications — Allow notifications for rest timers, workout controls and progress updates?
- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
- Affiliate referral — If an affiliate referred you, enter a promo code.
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google or email to create your account.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
- Registration — Confirm your password.
- Email verification — Enter the 4-digit code sent to your email, or verify using an email link.
