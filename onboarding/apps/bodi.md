# BODi

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The fitness questionnaire is followed by account creation. Notifications are requested after the subscription offer.

- Fitness level — What's your current fitness level?
- Workout preferences — What types of workouts do you enjoy?
- Workout duration — How much time can you commit on workout days?
- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password that meets the displayed requirements.
- Notifications — Would you like progress reminders, milestone badges and daily nudges?
