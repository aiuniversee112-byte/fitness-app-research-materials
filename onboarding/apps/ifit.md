# iFIT

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — registration and account checks

English translations of Russian form labels. No fitness-goal questionnaire is shown before the first home screen.

- Registration — Choose Sign up or Log in.
- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
- Registration — Confirm your password.
- Location — What is your country or region?
- Contact details — What is your phone number?
- Basic profile — What is your date of birth?
- Consent — Do you accept the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy?
- Email preferences — Would you like email updates, discounts and offers from iFIT?
- Verification — Complete the human-verification check.
- Promo code — Do you have a promo code?
- Membership — Choose your membership: iFIT Pro or iFIT Train.
