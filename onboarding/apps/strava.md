# Strava

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The recorded email route uses a verification code. Privacy and heatmap explanations are informational screens, not additional questionnaire answers.

- Registration — Choose Google, Apple or email to create your account.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Email verification — Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email address.
- Public profile — What is your first name?
- Public profile — What is your last name?
- Basic profile — When's your birthday?
- Gender — What's your gender?
- Activity preferences — What types of activities do you like to do?
- Goals — What do you plan to use Strava for?
- Fitness level — Where are you in your fitness journey?
- Notifications — Allow notifications about followers, Clubs and activity?
- Email preferences — Would you like emails about new features, challenges and updates?
- Community — Find friends or choose suggested accounts to follow.

### Appllama recording — first-activity setup

Shown after the final welcome screen.

- Activity tracking — Record an activity with Strava or connect a device to upload activities.
