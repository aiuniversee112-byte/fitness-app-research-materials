# SHRED

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The route selects Get Bigger, then edits a lower target weight. The summary preserves the prompts actually shown, including the resulting weight-loss pace question.

- Basic profile — Choose the male or female training experience.
- Body data — What's your current height?
- Body data — What's your current weight?
- Basic profile — What's your age?
- Training skill — How skilled do you feel when working out?
- Fitness goal — What's your fitness goal?
- Weight goal — What's your target weight?
- Goal pace — How fast do you want to lose the selected amount of weight?
- Training frequency — How many days per week do you work out?
- Recent activity — When was your last time in the gym?
- Gym type — What type of gym do you use?
- Home setup — How much equipment do you have in your home gym?
- Workout format — Choose your preferred workout format.
- Equipment — Choose your available equipment.
- Coaching style — What is your preferred coach style?
- Attribution — How did you hear about us?
- Notifications — Enable push notifications for coaching and workout updates?
- Apple Health — Allow SHRED to access and update your Health data?
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google or email to save your settings.
- Communications — Would you like to receive communications from SHRED?
- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
- Community — Choose recommended users to follow, or skip.
