# Technogym

## Onboarding questions

### User recording — registration and Fitness program setup

The route creates a new account and then follows Fitness → Stay Young. Sport and Health & Lifestyle are offered but their questionnaires are not entered.

- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
- Registration — What is your phone number? (Optional)
- Marketing preferences — Would you like personalized communications about services, products and benefits?
- Profile import — Would you like to fill out your profile with Apple Health?
- Basic profile — What is your date of birth?
- Basic profile — What is your gender?
- Body data — What is your weight?
- Body data — What is your height?
- Health-data consent — Do you consent to processing information about your state of health?
- Notifications — Would you like reminders about when to train and when to take a day off?
- Apple Health permissions — Allow Technogym to read health data and write activity data?
- Tracking permission — Allow Technogym to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites?
- Program setup — Would you like to create your tailored program now?
- Program category — What makes you move: Fitness, Sport or Health & Lifestyle?
- Fitness goal — What's your goal?
- Training experience — What's your training experience?
- Workout duration — How long do you want to work out? Each duration comes with a linked weekly frequency.
- Workout format — How do you prefer to train: trainer-led sessions or self-paced routines?
- Equipment — What equipment do you want to use?

### User recording — first-workout setup and feedback

Shown after the personalized program is created.

- Heart-rate tracking — Would you like to connect a heart-rate device?
- Workout feedback — How do you feel after the workout?
- Workout feedback — Do you have any suggestions to share?
