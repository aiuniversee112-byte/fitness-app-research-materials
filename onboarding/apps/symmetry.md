# Symmetry

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The recorded route includes a series of self-assessment statements, equipment setup and a commitment gesture. Informational Bodyrank demonstrations are omitted from the question list.

- Fitness goal — What is your top fitness goal?
- Motivation — Why do you want to achieve this goal?
- Obstacles — What is stopping you from achieving this goal?
- Training experience — How experienced are you with working out?
- Body data — What's your current weight?
- Basic profile — What is your biological gender?
- Attribution — How did you find us?
- Body image — Do you relate to this statement: “I really want to change my physique, but so far I haven’t been able to”?
- Consistency — Do you relate to this statement: “I haven’t been able to train consistently without quitting”?
- Training confidence — Do you relate to this statement: “I feel lost when I go to train and don’t really know what to do”?
- Exercise technique — Do you relate to this statement: “I do exercises with constant doubt about whether my technique is correct”?
- Training environment — What equipment do you have available?
- Equipment confirmation — Is this your equipment? Confirm or adjust the inventory.
- Training frequency — How many times a week do you want to work out?
- Schedule — If choosing specific days, which days would you like to train?
- Notifications — Allow reminders and notifications to help you stay focused on your goals?
- Email preferences — Can we send you emails?
- Workout duration — How long do you want workouts to be?
- Pain and injuries — Do you have any pain or injuries?
- Commitment — Hold to sign your promise to work out at the selected weekly frequency.
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google or email to create your account.
- Registration — Choose a username.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
- Registration — Confirm your password.
