# Muscle Booster

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — 34-step version

The counter includes informational slides. The user selects No for a special occasion, so the event-date question is not shown in this branch.

- Gender identity — Which gender do you identify with?
- Fitness goal — What is your goal: muscle gain or weight loss?
- Current physique — Choose your current body type.
- Body image — Do you like your body?
- Target physique — Choose your target body type.
- Target areas — Choose your target zones.
- Body image — When was the last time you were happy with your body image?
- Motivation — What motivates you to exercise?
- Priorities — Choose your main priorities.
- Fitness level — What is your fitness level?
- Strength baseline — How many push-ups can you do?
- Lifestyle — Do you have a sedentary lifestyle?
- Daily activity — How much do you walk daily?
- Sleep — How much sleep do you get?
- Hydration — How much water do you drink throughout the day?
- Energy — How do you feel between meals?
- Diet — Do you follow any of these diets?
- Habits — Are you an organized person?
- Body data — How tall are you?
- Body data — What is your current weight?
- Weight goal — What is your target weight?
- Basic profile — How old are you?
- Training location — Choose your workout location.
- Gym setup — What type of gym do you belong to?
- Goal pace — At what pace do you want to move towards your goal?
- Schedule — Choose your workout days.
- Goal deadline — Is there a special occasion you want to gain muscle for?
