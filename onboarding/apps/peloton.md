# Peloton

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — email registration

This recording contains account setup rather than a fitness questionnaire. Apple and Google are offered as alternative sign-up methods; their forms are not shown.

- Registration — Choose how to sign up: email, Apple or Google.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Email preferences — Would you like email updates about workout progress, milestones, features and products?
- Registration — Set a password with at least 8 characters.
- Public profile — Choose a username (3–15 characters, without special characters).
- Age confirmation — What is your date of birth?
