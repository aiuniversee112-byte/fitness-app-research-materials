## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The recorded branch is male and weight loss. The knee-pain severity question is visible after selecting Yes; it is retained even though the answer is later changed to No. Two extra questions appear during plan generation.

- Basic profile — What's your gender?
- Basic profile — How old are you?
- Body data — How tall are you?
- Body data — What's your weight?
- Fitness goal — What's your goal?
- Weight goal — What's your target weight?
- Body composition — What's your body composition?
- Body composition — What's your target composition?
- Target areas — What do you want to focus on?
- Training experience — Do you have any previous workout experience?
- Fitness level — How fit are you?
- Daily activity — How active are you?
- Knee pain — Do you suffer from knee pain?
- Knee pain severity — If yes, how serious are your problems?
- Strength baseline — How many squats can you do without a break?
- Strength baseline — How many push-ups can you do without a break?
- Strength baseline — How long can you hold a plank without resting?
- Training confidence — Does this sound like you: “I have no idea which workouts are right for me”?
- Daily activity — How much do you walk daily?
- Habits — Do you have any of these bad habits?
- Lifestyle — How satisfied are you with your current lifestyle?
- Consistency — Does this sound like you: “I easily give up when the workout feels too hard or boring”?
- Weight history — When was the last time you were at your ideal weight?
- Sleep — How much do you sleep every night?
- Morning routine — How long does it take you to get up from bed?
- Morning energy — How do you usually feel after you wake up?
- Motivation — Does this sound like you: “I often need external motivation to keep going”?
- Stress — How often do you feel stressed?
- Motivation — What motivates you the most?
- Training environment — Do you prefer a calm, private workout setting to a crowded gym?
- Goal confirmation — Do you want to lose weight?
- Goal confirmation — Do you want to get an attractive body?
- Goal confirmation — Do you want to prevent chronic disease?
- Training location — Where will you work out?
- Home workouts — Should we exclude or limit loud exercises at home?
- Goal deadline — Do you have any important event coming up?
- Commitment — For how long are you ready to commit?
- Workout duration — What length of workouts do you prefer?
- Name — What should we call you?
- Readiness — How motivated are you to start?
- Goal pace — What pace would you prefer in your plan?
- Schedule — Pick your training days.
- Reminders — Would you like workout reminders and notifications?
- Professional referral — Did a professional refer you to Fitify?
- Apple Health — Connect Apple Health and allow the required write permissions?
- Energy check — Do you usually feel tired around lunchtime?
- Daily activity — Are you typically on your feet or sitting most of the day?
- App feedback — Would you like to rate Fitify?
- Registration — Choose Apple, Google, Facebook or email to create your account.
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
