# Lyfta

## Onboarding questions

### Appllama recording — initial onboarding

The recording changes several answers and opens multiple equipment and reminder states. Each underlying prompt appears once below.

- Basic profile — What's your gender?
- Fitness goals — What are your goals?
- Primary use — What would you like to do most?
- Attribution — How did you hear about Lyfta?
- Motivation — Why do you track your workouts?
- Training experience — How experienced are you with weightlifting?
- Training frequency — How many days a week would you like to work out?
- Commitment — How long can you stick to it?
- Reminders — Which days and times would you like workout reminders?
- Notifications — Allow Lyfta to send notifications?
- Equipment — What equipment do you have?
- Apple Health — Allow Lyfta to access and update your Health data?
- Body data — How much do you weigh?
- Referral code — Enter a referral code, or skip this optional step.
- Registration — Continue with Apple or email.
- Registration — What is your first name?
- Registration — What is your last name?
- Registration — What is your email address?
- Registration — Create a password.
